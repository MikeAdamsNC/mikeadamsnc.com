# MikeAdamsNC brand brief

Paste this into Claude Design to start the brand. Anything marked (check) is a guess
Mike should confirm or fix.

## Who

Mike Adams, North Carolina. A dad of two who builds things for his own life because
he wants them to exist: an app that coaches his fishing, a display on the kitchen fridge
that runs the household, and a personal data system that keeps his whole history in one
place he owns. He tinkers with the home itself too: a weather station, trail cameras on
his land, a smart grill, the pool. (check)

## What the brand is for

A personal site, mikeadamsnc.com, that shows what he has built, says who he is, and
invites anyone to pitch him an idea. It is not a business and not a résumé. His day job
never appears.

## Personality (check)

- Builder, not salesman. Shows the thing, skips the hype.
- Plain words. Direct. A little dry humor.
- Practical and homegrown: things built for real life, at a real house, in North
  Carolina.
- Curious: will try building almost anything once.
- Generous with ideas, honest about time: "I read everything; I pick a few."

## Audience

Anyone: friends and family, neighbors, local businesses, strangers who find a project
online. Nobody should need to be technical to understand the site.

## Feel

Warm, handmade, confident. Closer to a well-kept workshop than a tech startup. Works in
light and dark.

## Avoid

- Startup and agency clichés: gradients, glowing blobs, "innovate," "solutions."
- Corporate polish that hides the person.
- Long dashes in copy (use commas, periods, or colons).

## Deliverables

1. Logo: a wordmark for "MikeAdamsNC" plus a small mark that works as a favicon and app
   icon. 2-3 directions to choose from.
2. Colors: a small palette (background, ink, muted, one or two accents), light and dark.
3. Type: a headline face and a body face, both free web fonts.
4. Voice: 5-6 sample lines (site headline, a project card, the idea-form intro, the
   "thanks, I got it" message).
5. One sample page: the home page showing three project cards (StartFishing, the fridge
   display, Archer), a short "about," and the "Got an idea?" invitation.

## Starting point

The current coming-soon page uses warm paper (#f4f1ea), near-black ink (#1c1b19),
Hanken Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono. Keep it, change it, or throw it out.
